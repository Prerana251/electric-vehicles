# Electric Vehicle (EV) Data Analysis
Project Overview
This project analyzes electric vehicle (EV) adoption patterns using
vehicle registration data from 2011 to 2023. The analysis explores EV
types, manufacturers, models, model-year trends, CAFV (Clean Alternative
Fuel Vehicle) eligibility, and state-wise distribution.

The objective is to turn vehicle registration data into actionable
insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
Business Questions
- How many electric vehicles are represented in the dataset?
- What is the split between Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and
  Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs)?
- How has EV adoption changed over time?
- Which manufacturers and models have the highest number of
  registrations?
- What proportion of vehicles are CAFV eligible?
- How are EV registrations distributed across states?

Dashboard Visualizations
- EVs by model year: Line/area chart showing registration trends
  from 2011 to 2023.
- EVs by state: Map showing the geographic distribution of
  registrations.
- Top manufacturers: Bar chart comparing leading EV makes.
- CAFV eligibility: Donut chart showing eligible, not eligible,
  and unknown categories.
- Top EV models: Treemap/table highlighting the most registered
  models.
- Interactive filters: EV type, state, CAFV eligibility, and
  model.
Key Insights
- BEVs account for 77.8% of the EVs in the analysis, compared with
  22.2% for PHEVs.
- Registrations increased from approximately 0.8K in 2011 to 37.1K in
  2023.
- Tesla is the leading manufacturer in the report, with 68,939
  vehicles (57.6%).
- Tesla Model Y (28,501) and Tesla Model 3 (27,708) are the two
  leading models.
- CAFV eligibility is recorded as unknown for 46.35% of vehicles,
  eligible for 41.87%, and not eligible for 11.77%.
- The report identifies California as the state with the largest
  number of registrations.

Business Recommendations
- Encourage a wider range of EV models and price points to support
  broader adoption.
- Continue improving battery range and communicate range information
  clearly to consumers.
- Investigate the large unknown CAFV eligibility category to improve
  data completeness.
- Use state-level adoption patterns to identify regions where EV
  infrastructure and awareness may need support.
